# Roadmap - 2026-10-29

Written after the 2026-10-29 audit: two same-family auditors plus a cross-model (codex) pass found 29
issues, and all of them are fixed on `overhaul/2026-10-29`. The fixes include server speech consent,
capture restart after a dead stream, Deepgram utterance handling, the untrusted-transcript fence, Keychain
state, and the hardened release workflow. The test count went from 191 to 392.

This is what comes next, in priority order. Every item is test-first and keeps the privacy promise in
CONTRIBUTING.md: nothing leaves the Mac except what the welcome screen describes.

## Now (this release)

### M1. Capture health you can see
**Problem.** Some failures still show only a problem line while the status keeps saying "Listening":
- a transcriber that refuses to start or gives up (a rejected Deepgram key, a Mac without on-device
  dictation);
- a stream that dies without calling back;
- a side that captures silence.

**Shape.**
- `LiveTranscriber` gains a fatal signal. The controller marks that side failed and the status says so.
- A watchdog notices a side with no audio buffers for 30 s while capture is up, and a side with audio
  energy but no text for 60 s. It restarts that side through the existing backoff chain, then reports.
- A small per-side level indicator in the overlay shows at a glance that both sides are being heard.

### M2. Listen to the meeting app only
**Problem.** System audio capture takes everything the Mac plays: music, notification sounds, a video
in another tab. That pollutes "Them" and wastes speech recognition.

**Shape.**
- A Settings choice: "All apps", or one running app (Zoom, Teams, Webex, FaceTime, Slack, a browser).
- It is implemented with an application-filtered `SCContentFilter`.
- If the chosen app is not running at Start, the app falls back to all apps and says so.

### M3. Transcript safety net
**Problem.** The transcript lives only in memory. A crash, a quit or a forgotten Copy loses the meeting.

**Shape.**
- Opt-in "Save transcripts" in Settings (off by default) with a folder picker. The default folder is
  `~/Documents/Meeting Assistant`.
- When on, the transcript is written as Markdown with timestamps every 30 s and on Stop. Files are
  owner-only (0600).
- A "Save Transcript..." menu item works at any time.
- Files never leave the Mac. PRIVACY.md says where they go.

### M4. Meeting summary
**Problem.** After the call you still have to turn the transcript into notes.

**Shape.**
- A "Summarize Meeting" action, available while listening and after Stop. It sends the whole transcript,
  fenced as untrusted, to Claude and shows a summary with decisions and action items.
- The summary can be copied, and saved next to the transcript when saving is on.
- This sends the full transcript rather than recent excerpts, so the welcome screen, `termsVersion` and
  PRIVACY.md change in the same release.

### M5. Never drop a question
**Problem.** A question detected while an answer is still streaming is dropped by the throttle.

**Shape.** Keep the most recent pending question. When the current answer finishes, answer it if it is
still recent (within 30 s). Otherwise drop it with a quiet note in the overlay.

### M6. Long-meeting bounds
**Problem.** The transcript is unbounded in memory, and every update re-renders everything after the
oldest open turn.

**Shape.**
- Keep a bounded number of finished turns in memory (the prompt only ever needs the tail).
- When saving is on, older turns are already on disk; when it is off, the overlay says older lines were
  trimmed.
- A test runs a synthetic three-hour meeting and asserts flat memory.

### M7. Testable app shell
**Problem.** Terms resume, overlay toggle and hotkey binding live in `MeetingAssistantApp.swift`, which
the test target excludes.

**Shape.**
- Move that logic into an `AppCoordinator` that tests drive through fakes.
- The status line names the active speech backend and the Claude model.
- Refresh the README: the Releasing section (secrets in the `release` environment, all-or-nothing
  signing, bump `VERSION` and `MARKETING_VERSION` together) and the overlay screenshot.

## Later

- Speaker diarization on the "Them" side (several remote participants).
- A local LLM option for answers, so nothing leaves the Mac at all.
- Calendar-aware start: offer to start listening when a meeting with a call link begins.
- Mac App Store build (sandboxed ScreenCaptureKit), which would end the per-update Keychain and
  permission prompts that ad-hoc releases cause.
